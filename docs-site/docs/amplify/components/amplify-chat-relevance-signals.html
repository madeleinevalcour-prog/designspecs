Why Amplify picked this record. The Amplify icon and a label, then one small chip per signal, inside a 1px Amplify-gradient border. It’s the content slot of a Prospect contact card.
PartWhat it is
Amplify iconThe filled Amplify icon, 12px, in the Amplify radial
Labelinput/value/sm in color/text/body. “Relevance Signals” by default
ChipsOne small Novo Chip per signal, spacing/gap/xxsm apart. They wrap onto more lines when there are many
BorderA 1px Amplify gradient, color/amplify/amplify-green → amplify-blue, radius border/radius/sm, padding spacing/padding/sm
  • Signals are short and factual: “Director-level”, “Department Match: Project Management”, “Previously contacted”.
  • Relevance signals belong to Prospect. Relevancy dots belong to candidate search. They never appear in the same reply.
  • Relevance signals can contribute to a “Strong Match”, depending on the context.

“Relevance Signals” is the default. The label input sets a contextual label, for example “Hiring signals” in the Adding prospect contacts flow.

Keep the label short, and use it to describe why the record was picked. Write a long label, or one that doesn’t say why the record was picked.

In a Cards stack, prospect-contact cards fill their content slot with relevance signals from each item’s signals.

The prospect add-flow mockup gives an added card’s signals a subtle background. The component has no such state yet.
  • The Amplify icon is decorative (aria-hidden); the label carries the meaning.
  • The label and chips are plain text. The chips aren’t interactive, so they’re not in the tab order.
