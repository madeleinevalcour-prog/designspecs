The recruiter’s chat history on the Amplify tab. It opens from the Chats button in the Amplify header and sits on the right of the page: scheduled prompts first, then past chats by when they were last used.

Same container as the prompt card and chat-list-item: padding 8 / 16, radius 8, the same border, fill and states, so every Amplify card reads as one family (Gestalt similarity). It has its own component tokens. There is no kebab. Order, top to bottom:

  • item-header: the title (body/lg-medium, 1 line, truncates), then the caption on the right — the last activity (meta/sm), or the “N new” indicator chip (NovoChip, positive, small) for unread scheduled runs.
  • item-data: on a chat, the record it’s about: the entity icon in its entity color (color/entity/job, color/entity/contact, color/entity/company…) and the record name. On a scheduled chat, the frequency and time window (clock-arrow), plus the status when it isn’t active.
  • description: body/default, 2 lines, then truncates. Chats only.

  • Scheduled chats are listed only under Scheduled, not again in the history.
  • Groups sit 16 apart and cards 8 apart, so each group reads as one unit (Gestalt proximity).
  • Search filters as you type: scheduled names, chat titles, descriptions and record names.
  • While searching, Scheduled lists every match, not just the first 3.
  • No matches shows the empty-state (In container=no): search icon, “No chats match “query””, “Try a different word.”
  • The field shows its active state (focus border, clear button) while it has text. Clear or Esc empties it and keeps focus in the field.
