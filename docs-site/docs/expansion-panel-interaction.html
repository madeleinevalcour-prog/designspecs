How an expansion panel opens and closes in response to input, and what the panel does at each step. Scoped to behavior only.

An expansion panel has two resting states — collapsed and expanded — and toggles between them from a single control. Only the transition and the panel’s response are documented here.

Trigger

Activating the panel header toggles the panel: a collapsed panel expands, an expanded panel collapses. The whole header row is the target, not just the caret. The caret indicator rotates to reflect the new state.

On expand

  • The content region grows from zero height to the natural height of its content.
  • Content below the panel reflows down to make room; it is not overlaid.
  • The caret rotates to its open orientation.

On collapse

  • The content region shrinks back to zero height and is removed from the layout.
  • Content below reflows up to close the gap.
  • The caret returns to its closed orientation.
Whether panels in a group are independent or mutually exclusive (accordion) is a group-level configuration, not part of a single panel’s expand/collapse behavior.

The height change and caret rotation are animated together so the panel reads as one motion. The exact values were not read from the live component and are unresolved here.
