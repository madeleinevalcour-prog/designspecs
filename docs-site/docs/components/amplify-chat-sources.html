The sources row sits under an Amplify reply that used records, so the recruiter can see what the answer is based on. A one-line summary, “Based on 14 job orders · Updated today”, expands to the records themselves, each a small link with its entity-color circle.

The rules for records and sources are in Chat interface patterns. That page wins where the two differ.

  • The toggle is a normal button: Tab to reach it, Enter or Space to expand or collapse.
  • A source with a link opens the record. A source without one renders as a button and hands the click to the host, for example to open a preview.
  • Expanded is two-way bindable ([(expanded)]); once the toggle is used or expanded is bound, it overrides state.
  • Under the reply, spacing/gap/md (16) from it; follow-up chips sit spacing/gap/sm (8) below the row.
 Show a sources row when the answer depends on specific records or fields. Show it for clarifying questions, general product help or “nothing found” replies. Label each source by record name, with its entity circle. List bare IDs, or rebuild the circle and restyle the link. Give prospect contacts that aren’t in the ATS the neutral grey circle, and saved ones the contact color. Color an unsaved prospect as a contact. The color says the record exists in the ATS.
  • Say what the answer is based on and how fresh it is: “Based on 14 job orders · Updated today”.
  • Use the recruiting name for the records (job orders, candidates, contacts), or “records” for a mix.
  • Name the fields a ranking used in the reply, for example “Ranked by start date, submissions, and client priority”. Don’t claim a reason the data doesn’t support.
  • Show an ID as meta text only when it tells two sources apart, such as two “PepsiCo” entries.
