A general term for menu items or overflow experiences revealed on click: a card that holds one or more option groups. Use it to pick one action, choose a value, or tick several values in a check-list. It works with any trigger.

ComponentWhat it is
Dropdown + triggerThe card, positioned under its trigger, and the directive that opens it
Option groupA group of rows: options (default) or checkboxes (check-list)
OptionOne row: optional icon, label, check when selected

The card: dropdown/color/* background and border, dropdown/border-radius, general/level 4, 8 padding and option groups 8 apart. At least 180 wide; otherwise as wide as its longest option.

SettingUse
role listbox (default)Choosing a value. Options become role="option" with aria-selected
role menuRunning an action. Options become role="menuitem"
placement bottom-start · bottom-endUnder the trigger, 4 below it (dropdown/spacing/gap), left- or right-aligned. inline (default) keeps it in the flow, for docs

The trigger is any element, usually a button, with the trigger directive. It sets aria-haspopup, aria-expanded and aria-controls, and toggles the dropdown on click.

A group of rows inside a dropdown. Two types are built.

TypeUse
defaultA column of options, no gap. One choice or one action
check-listA column of checkbox labels. Several values at once; Space toggles
  • A group label is optional and not part of the Figma component: subtle body/sm medium text above the rows. It also names the group for screen readers.

One row: an optional 16px icon, the label in input/value/default, and a check on the right when selected. 8 padding, 8 between icon and label, radius border/radius/xsm.

  • The option keeps its native element (button, list item or div), so its semantics stay. Use a button for actions.
  • Clicking the trigger toggles the dropdown. Clicking outside it (not on the trigger) closes it.
  • Choosing an option reports its value and, by default, closes the dropdown and returns focus to the trigger. Turn closeOnChoose off for check-lists or multi-step choices.
  • Options aren’t in the tab order; the arrow keys move focus between them.

Escape is marked as handled, so a dropdown inside a pop-over or dialog closes only itself; the enclosing Amplify pop-over stays open.

 Use role="menu" for actions and the default listbox for choosing a value. Use a check-list group when the recruiter can pick several values, and keep the dropdown open while they tick.   • The panel is role="listbox" or role="menu"; options are option (with aria-selected) or menuitem. Disabled options have aria-disabled.
  • The trigger carries aria-haspopup, aria-expanded and aria-controls.
  • Focus moves into the dropdown on open from the keyboard and returns to the trigger on Escape or after a choice.
  • Option groups are role="group", named by their label when there is one.
  • Keyboard focus shows the hover fill plus a 2px color/border/focus ring, inset.
